# Blocks and Decorations

You can decorate your house for [HallowNight]($hallownight), leave out Treats for visitors, or set up a proper Witch’s brewing room. Some of these blocks appear in villages during the festival, so keep an eye on the houses and the village square!

## HallowNight Decorations

| Decoration | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Ghost Sheet Lantern | A two-block-tall ghost that lights up its surroundings. Make one with a Ghost Sheet and a Lantern. Stand it on the floor or hang it from the ceiling. |
| Scarecrow | A decoration crafted with an Armour Stand surrounded by eight Wheat. Give it a helmet, chestplate, pumpkin, mob head or [Boss Trophy]($bosses) to dress it up. |
| Spooky Bunting | Hang little spooky shapes on a cord between two points. Make four with three String and one Paper. |
| Spooky Wall Decal | Adds a spooky design to the side of a block. The inventory icon previews the design you’ll place next. |
| Carved Pumpkin | Use a [Pumpkin Carving Knife]($items) on a pumpkin to make and save your own designs. |
| Gravestones and Tombstones | Decorate a graveyard, garden or doorstep. Check the Stonecutter for the different shapes. |

### Hanging Spooky Bunting

Crouch and right-click to place the first anchor on a wall. Keep crouching and place the second anchor where you want the other end to hang. You can also crouch-click an existing anchor to select it and join on another length of bunting.

### Treat Bowls and Doorbells

Craft a **Treat Bowl** with a Bowl, Sugar and any item accepted as a Treat. To host visitors, put your own bowl outside with at least two Treats, leave a clear route to it and stay within 32 blocks during a HallowNight evening. A Doorbell is optional for hosting. You can also find Treat Bowls outside village libraries during the event.

Village Doorbells can be used to call a Villager during HallowNight. Check the [festival guide]($hallownight) for what to wear and how to collect a Treat.

## Useful Blocks

### Witch’s Caldron

Craft a Witch’s Caldron with five Iron Ingots and a Glass Bottle. Heat it from below to brew Potions, make [Holy Water]($items), purify iron into [Silver]($weapons), or work with a Restless Soul.

You can also pour a finished potion straight into a Caldron for storage. It holds up to three bottles of the same potion and form (drinkable, splash or lingering). Use empty Glass Bottles to take it back out. Storing potions doesn’t require heat.

### Coffins

A Coffin lets you sleep through daylight, which is especially useful if you’re a Vampire. Crouch and right-click to open or close the lid.
