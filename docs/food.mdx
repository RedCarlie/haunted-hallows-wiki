# Food and Treats

Haunted Hallows adds plenty of sweets to collect, as well as crops you can grow at home. Some Treats are just a nice snack, while others can give you a Potion Effect or even leave you with a Curse!

## Treats

### How to Find Treats

The easiest way to try a few different sweets is to go [Trick-or-Treating]($hallownight) in a costume. Eligible mobs wearing pumpkins can drop Treats during HallowNight, and a [Treat Pickaxe]($weapons) gives you another way to find sweets while mining. You can also make Peppermint, Toffee Apples, Lollipops, Jawbreakers and Candy Corn, among others. Check your Recipe Book as you pick up new ingredients!

Try eating your finds and checking the Treat Collection in your [Hallow Journal]($items). A few sweets have effects of their own, so it’s worth trying something you haven’t seen before.

### Treats with Special Effects

- **Jelly Beans** can take on Potion Effects. Toss one into a potion-filled [Witch’s Caldron]($blocks) to infuse it.
- **Spooky Squishies** can turn up while Trick-or-Treating or as drops from Ghosts.
- **Gummy Vampire Teeth** can be made using Vampire Blood. Eating them can give you Vampirism.
- **Werewolf Biscuits** can be found in [Werewolf boss loot]($bosses). Eating one can give you Lycanthropy and set up a rematch, so save it for when you actually want another fight!

## Crops and Cooking

### Corn

Plant Corn Kernels on farmland to grow your own corn. Keep some Kernels for your next crop, or cook them into Popcorn in a Smoker. If you find corn growing in a generated Corn Maze, follow the paths and look for the chest!

### Garlic and Garlic Bread

Look for Garlic in Cemetery and Corn Maze chests, including in the Endless Maize. Crows also have a small chance to drop it. Plant a bulb on farmland and harvest it when it’s mature (save a bulb to plant again!).

Garlic is used to brew [Holy Water]($items). Combine it with Bread to make **Garlic Bread**, which gives you a brief dose of Purified. This can protect you from a new infection while it’s active. A Vampire can eat Garlic Bread, but Purified still hurts them. Raw Garlic poisons Vampires.

## Food for the Cursed

Vampires feed on [Blood]($items) instead of ordinary food. If you’re in wolf form, eat raw meat or hunt mobs to keep your hunger up. If you’re looking for a cure, see [Bosses and Curses]($bosses).
