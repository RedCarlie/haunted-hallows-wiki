# HallowNight

For this Mod, a Minecraft "year" is 96 days. When first creating a world, it starts at 56 Days until the HallowNight event, which itself lasts 7 days(Day 57-63). Afterwards, you will need to wait another Minecraft Year length (96 Days) until the next HallowNight. 
This time will automatically link with Mid-Autumn in "Serene Seasons" if that mod is Installed. This can be changed in the Config.

A live Countdown for this event will appear in the first page of the Hallow Journal, right above the Moon Phase.

### What Happens During Hallow Night?

Hallow Night being active both changes and allows for:
- Seasonal Mob Spawns and Appearances; including pumpkin-headed mobs.
- Special Mob Drops
- Village Decorations and Music; Decorations around houses and the village jukebox.
- Trick-or-Treating
- A Secret Boss Battle
- Enables Other Mod Events; optional compatibility with mods such as Born in Chaos and Deep Aether.
