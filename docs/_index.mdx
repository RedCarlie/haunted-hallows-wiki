# Welcome to Haunted Hallows 

Haunted Hallows is a 1.20.1 forge server and client based, open world Adventure mod which is focussed entirely on Halloween and allowing the player to take part in mysteries as well as fictional and real Halloween traditions. It has content ranging from Boss Fights, Music, New Structures, New Food and Crops, Items and Weapons, Dimensions, Potions, Decorations and Events! 

Content Warning: Demonic Themes, Blood and References to Death. This Wiki is not finished, so do not expect all of the Mod's information to be here yet.

This mod includes compatibility with: Farmer's Delight, Create, Corn Delight, J.E.R, Born in Chaos, The Aether & Deep Aether, Twilight Forest, Umbral Skies, Alex's Caves & Alex's Caves Enriched, TConstuct & Tinkers' Compatibility, Curios, Ice and Fire, Alex's Mobs, and more to come in the future.
